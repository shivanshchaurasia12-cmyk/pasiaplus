SIP for Beginners: Start With Rs 500

  • SIP means putting a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month.
  • You can start with Rs 500 on apps like Groww or Zerodha Coin.
  • Choose an index fund if you are new.
  • Stay invested for 5 years or more.
  • Do not stop your SIP when the market falls.

This is general information, not financial advice. Read the fund details before investing.
