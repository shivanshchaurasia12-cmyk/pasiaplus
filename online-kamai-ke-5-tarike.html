5 Ways to Earn Online From Home in India

  • Blogging: write useful posts and earn from ads once visitors come.
  • Freelancing: sell writing, design or editing on Fiverr or Upwork.
  • Selling on Meesho: list products and ship with zero shop rent.
  • Tutoring: teach school subjects on video calls.
  • Reselling: buy cheap, sell with a small profit on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Start with one method. Give it 90 days before judging it.
