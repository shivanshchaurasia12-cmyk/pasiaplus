7 UPI Safety Tips to Avoid Fraud

  • Never share your UPI PIN with anyone. You do not need a PIN to receive money.
  • Ignore 'collect request' messages you did not expect.
  • Check the receiver name before you pay.
  • Do not scan QR codes to receive money.
  • Install apps only from the official Play Store or App Store.
  • Report fraud on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in quickly.

If someone is rushing you to pay, stop and think.
