How to Save Money Every Month: Simple Rules

  • Pay yourself first: move 10% of income to savings on salary day.
  • Track every expense for 30 days in a notebook or app.
  • Cut one subscription you do not use.
  • Set a weekly cash limit for shopping and eating out.
  • Keep 3 months of expenses as an emergency fund.

Small steady savings beat big plans you never start.
